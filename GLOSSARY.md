# Techstyles

A workspace where a fashion brand founder defines their brand, develops products as tech packs, and takes them through sourcing to production.

## Language

### Brand

**Founder**:
The person who runs a fashion brand and works in its Workspace. Techstyles is built for founders in general; one founder per Workspace for now.
_Avoid_: User, owner, customer

**Workspace**:
One brand's space in Techstyles: its Brand Profile, products, libraries and sourcing. It goes by the Brand Profile's name, or "My workspace" until one is set.
_Avoid_: Studio, account, Local Studio

**Brand Profile**:
The brand's shared identity (name, logo, values, audience, tone, colors) that designs and marketing draw on.
_Avoid_: Brand settings, brand kit

### Products

**Tech Pack**:
The part of a product a manufacturer sees: its sections, shared by link, printed to PDF and saved in named versions.
_Avoid_: Spec sheet, product file

**Sourcing**:
The part of a product only the founder sees: its manufacturer, Quote Requests and Quotes. Never part of the tech pack.
_Avoid_: Procurement, purchasing

### Sourcing

**Manufacturer**:
The factory that makes a finished product from its tech pack.
_Avoid_: Supplier, factory, vendor (for the garment maker)

**Supplier**:
A vendor of fabrics or trims used in a product's materials, such as a mill or trim vendor.
_Avoid_: Manufacturer (for a fabric or trim source)

**Quote Request**:
One product's tech pack sent to one manufacturer to ask for a price; a product has at most one per manufacturer. It moves through Draft, Sent, Quoted, and ends Chosen or Declined.
_Avoid_: RFQ, inquiry, sourcing request

**Quote**:
A manufacturer's answer to a Quote Request: one unit price, MOQ and lead times for the whole product. Part of its Quote Request, not a record of its own.
_Avoid_: Offer, bid, estimate
